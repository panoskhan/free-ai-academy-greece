# Free AI Academy Greece — Product Positioning

## Purpose

Free AI Academy Greece is a free, Greek-first, practical open-learning project for people who want to understand AI and build useful digital projects.

## Primary audience

- Beginners who want a structured starting point
- Learners who want practical AI and web skills
- Creators who want portable projects they can inspect, export, publish, or self-host
- Learners who want evidence of what they actually built rather than passive course completion

## Core promise

**Learn → Build → Test → Document → Publish.**

The Academy should always prioritize demonstrable work over passive content consumption.

## Differentiation

The project should remain:

1. Free at its core
2. Greek-first and understandable
3. Project-based
4. Portable and open-source friendly
5. Verification-oriented
6. Privacy-conscious
7. Explicit about what is and is not accredited

## Product boundaries

Do not turn the project into a generic course marketplace, a paid-course funnel, or a credentialing claim without formal recognition.

The existing Builder, Portfolio, Assessment, Certificate and Release Center features should support the learning loop rather than become separate products competing for attention.

## Competitive context reviewed 2026-09-12

Current Greek/European AI-learning offerings show strong demand for practical, project-based AI education, but they vary substantially in audience and model. The Academy should therefore win on its specific combination of free access, Greek-first learning, portable project evidence and self-hosting/open-source orientation rather than trying to imitate commercial or institutional academies.

## Next product priority

Before adding another major feature, verify the public learner journey:

1. Discover the Academy
2. Understand who it is for
3. Start the first lesson
4. Complete a practical task
5. Record evidence
6. See progress
7. Continue to the next project

If any step is confusing, fix that before expanding the feature set.
